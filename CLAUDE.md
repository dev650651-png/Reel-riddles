# RULES: read before every task
FORMAT: vertical 1080x1920, 30 fps, Remotion. One folder per video in src/videos/<name>/, each registered in src/Root.tsx. Part 1 videos are 30-40 s and end 2 s after the voice.
ARCHITECTURE (every video): 1 HOOK (0-3 s): frame 0 is already moving, no fade-in or logo; a big title with a number and the stakes is on screen from frame 0; sound from 0 s. 2 BODY: the setup or first half of the value; the picture changes every 1-2 s. 3 RE-HOOK: a question or twist slams on screen. 4 FINAL BODY: the payoff (A-D answer tiles or the key point). 5 CLIFFHANGER: hold back the answer ("answer in the next reel"), ask for a comment, tap a Follow button at the end.
LOOK: on-screen text in Roman CAPITALS (Hinglish or English), 2-4 words per chip, Poppins 700/800 with a thick dark outline; never Devanagari on screen. Big chunky cartoon characters with thick outlines and big heads, always moving a little (breathing, blinking, swaying); they act out every line of the voice. Nothing covers a face. Keep key text out of the bottom 300 px and away from the right edge. Depth: background, middle and foreground layers, soft light, vignette, light grain. Real physics: gravity, bounce, squash and stretch, dust and splashes on impacts. Smooth camera moves inside a scene; hard cuts between scenes.
TIMING: every action, chip and sound lands on its spoken word, taken from public/words.json. Never guess times.
SOUND: the voice is always on top. Music about 10% while speaking and 18% in gaps; sound effects at 0.1-0.3. A whoosh on every cut, a pop on every chip, a matching sound on every action. Final loudness -14 LUFS.
QUALITY CHECK before every final render: a contact sheet of 12 stills at the key words. Check: hook frame moving, faces clear, text readable on a phone, actions on their words, nothing cut off, logic correct. Fix, check again, then render.
WORKING: one change per request, and a still to prove it. Keep NOTES.md up to date. Copyright: take only the idea from any reference. CLOUD: the disk is small, so delete renders and caches you no longer need; always send me finished videos and contact sheets as files; voice files arrive in voice/ in this repo; commit and push at the end of every session (node_modules, out and models in .gitignore).
