## Logic check
Paid 8,000 + 10,000 = 18,000. Received 9,000 + 11,000 = 20,000. Profit 2,000.
The "1,000 loss" on buying back is not money lost: the 10,000 is already counted once as spending. Counting it again as a loss
counts it twice.
